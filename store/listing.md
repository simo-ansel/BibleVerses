## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Every day BibleVerses gives you one verse and a painting made for that day. The verse is chosen from a hand-picked collection of 369 passages, and each day's painting is generated on your phone: a new pattern of watercolour washes and marbled pigment, never the same twice.

The whole Bible is inside, and it works offline.

THE COMPLETE COLLECTION
• The King James Version, Old and New Testaments
• The Apocrypha, as printed in the King James Bible of 1611: 1 and 2 Esdras, Tobit, Judith, the Additions to Esther, the Wisdom of Solomon, Sirach, Baruch, the Prayer of Azariah, Susanna, Bel and the Dragon, the Prayer of Manasseh, 1 and 2 Maccabees
• 1 Enoch and Jubilees, from the Bible of the Ethiopian Orthodox Church, in the translations of R. H. Charles
82 books and more than 39,000 verses.

READ
Browse book by book and chapter by chapter, and pick up where you left off.

SEARCH
Find every verse that contains a word or phrase across all 82 books, or only in the Apocrypha, the New Testament or the Ethiopian canon. Type a reference such as John 3:16, Sirach 43 or Enoch 1 to open it.

SAVE AND SHARE
Keep the verses you love in one place. Share any verse as an image with its painting, sized for stories.

A DAILY REMINDER
Choose a time and get a gentle reminder that today's verse is ready.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your saved verses stay on your phone.
