Privacy Policy

BibleVerses for Android. Effective 10 October 2026.

BibleVerses does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

The verses you save, your settings (notification time, typeface, text size) and the last chapter you read are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Notifications

If you turn on the morning notification, the app schedules local notifications on your phone. No server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.

Sharing

When you share a verse, the app creates an image on your phone and hands it to the app you choose. What happens next is governed by that app's own policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
